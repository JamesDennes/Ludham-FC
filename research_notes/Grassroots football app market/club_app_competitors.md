# Competitor landscape: club management / team apps for English grassroots football (UK pricing, 2025-2026)

Method note: research done 19 Nov 2026. Direct page fetches to vendor sites (pitchero.com, spond.com, teamer.net) were blocked by this session's network policy, so all figures below come from web search result extracts of vendor pages, help centres, app store listings and third-party comparison sites. "Date seen" = 19 Nov 2026 unless stated. Many comparison pages are written by competitors (Medalyst, ClubPal, Mingle Sport, BeeNet, Vanta, GrassrootsFC, SportMember, ClubZap, AppBuild) and are flagged as such. Verify every £ figure on the vendor's live pricing page before quoting it publicly.

## Pitchero (club websites + club app): plans, prices, sponsors, monetisation, complaints

### Takeaway
Pitchero is the default "club website + app" for multi-team English clubs: Free (1 team, carries third-party ads), Standard £38/month, Pro £99/month, with payment fees on top. It now also owns Teamer (closed Oct 2026) and is funnelling Teamer users onto Pitchero. Weak public sentiment (Trustpilot 2.5/5 on very few reviews), ads on the free tier and cost are the main criticisms.

### Cited Findings
- Plans: Free, Standard, Pro; paid plans have a 30-day free trial; Free is "ideal for single-team or smaller clubs", Standard for "growing clubs with multiple teams", Pro for "larger clubs needing advanced features" — [Pitchero pricing](https://www.pitchero.com/pricing); [Pitchero help: manage package](https://help.pitchero.com/knowledge/pitchero-club-website/packages/manage-package)
- Prices: Standard £38/month, Pro £99/month; Free limited to 1 team — [Pitchero help centre](https://help.pitchero.com/knowledge/pitchero-club-website/packages/manage-package); [GrassrootsFC "Pitchero Pricing 2026 — £38–£99/mo"](https://grassrootsfc.co.uk/vs-pitchero.html) (competitor page). Comparison pages say these reflect the pricing page as of July 2026 — [ClubPilot UK comparison](https://clubpilot.co.uk/best-club-management-software-uk)
- Team limits and payment fees (third-party): Standard up to 20 teams, payment fees from 2.42% + 17p; Pro unlimited teams, fees from 1.67% + 15p — [ClubPilot UK comparison](https://clubpilot.co.uk/best-club-management-software-uk) (unverified against Pitchero's own page)
- Additional teams £2.50/month; monthly or annual billing, annual cheaper — [Pitchero pricing](https://www.pitchero.com/pricing)
- US pricing differs: $50/month Standard, $130/month Pro — [Pitchero US package page](https://www.pitchero.com/en_US/club-website/choose-package)
- Ads/monetisation: Pitchero's own guides say paid packages bring "fewer or no" third-party ads; competitor sites say Standard only limits ads and full removal needs Pro; one cited user review flagged ads on youth club pages as inappropriate — [Pitchero free website guide](https://join.pitchero.com/free-website-for-sports-clubs); [Mingle Sport Pitchero alternative](https://mingle.sport/pitchero-alternative/); [ClubPal Pitchero alternative](https://www.clubpal.app/compare/pitchero-alternative)
- Payments on free tier: disputed — competitor pages say not available on Free; Pitchero's table lists "Payments" in core features — [Pitchero pricing](https://www.pitchero.com/pricing); [GrassrootsFC](https://grassrootsfc.co.uk/vs-pitchero.html)
- Fixtures/tables: CSV fixture import via control panel; manual league table builder; teams connected to a recognised competition in Pitchero's competition database get fixtures, results and tables pulled automatically; Play-Cricket has a daily sync — [Pitchero importing fixtures](https://www.pitchero.com/user-guides/club-website/teams/fixtures-results-tables/importing-fixtures); [Pitchero 3rd-party-powered competitions](https://help.pitchero.com/knowledge/3rd-party-powered-competitions); [Pitchero competitions](https://www.pitchero.com/features/competitions)
- Long-standing non-league football relationship ("15-year partnership") — [Pitchero non-league football](https://join.pitchero.com/non-league-football)
- Scale claims: 10,000+ clubs, 64,000+ teams, 1,235,000+ users (About page); LinkedIn says 60,000+ teams, 10,000 clubs, 1.5m active users — [Pitchero About](https://pitchero.com/about?amp=); [Pitchero LinkedIn](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/pitchero); [Pitchero case studies "Over 10,000 clubs"](https://www.pitchero.com/club-website/case-studies)
- Trustpilot UK: 8 reviews, TrustScore 2.5/5; negatives mostly billing (e.g. parents charged 3 times instead of once) — [Trustpilot UK Pitchero](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/www.pitchero.com). Do not confuse with PitcheroGPS (≈135 reviews, 4.5★) — [Trustpilot PitcheroGPS](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/pitcherogps.com)
- Pitchero Group owns Teamer and is shifting investment to Pitchero and PitcheroGPS; Teamer users offered a discount to move to Pitchero — [Teamer Shutdown FAQs](https://help.teamer.net/knowledge/teamer-shutdown-faqs); [Teamer: Moving to Pitchero](https://help.teamer.net/move-to-pitchero)
- Recommended in guides for multi-team clubs wanting website + app together — [Squadd blog](https://www.squadd.co.uk/blog/best-football-team-management-apps); [Spond top 5 grassroots apps](https://www.spond.com/news-and-blog/top-5-grassroots-football-team-apps/)

### Inferences
- A one-team Saturday/Sunday adult club wanting no ads on its public site faces ~£456/yr (Standard) to ~£1,188/yr (Pro) before payment fees. That is the main price anchor for a per-club PWA.
- Pitchero's sponsor features could not be confirmed in detail (see gaps); the ad-supported free tier means sponsor space competes with Pitchero's own ads, which is a selling point for an ad-free product with dedicated sponsor placements.
- Teamer closure creates a pool of displaced grassroots teams in late 2026.

### Gaps
- Exact feature split per tier (sponsor module, custom domain, app branding) not confirmed — pricing page not fetchable.
- No confirmed FA Full-Time auto-sync for football found; only "competition database" and CSV import.
- Player stats and match report tooling not confirmed from Pitchero sources.
- Very small Trustpilot sample; no Reddit threads surfaced.

## Spond: free model, revenue, football features

### Takeaway
Spond is free (no ads in core per its brand-partnership model) and makes money mainly from payment fees (UK 2.5% + 20p per transaction, via Stripe), fundraising (Superdraw: Spond keeps 35%) and brand partnerships. It is the most-used organiser in UK grassroots (claims 1.5m UK users) but is generic: no player stats, no match reports, no fines tooling found, no supporter-facing content.

### Cited Findings
- Free to download and use; only cost is a transaction fee if collecting money; Spond only makes money when groups process payments through Spond; Stripe handles transactions — [Spond help: payments costs](https://help.spond.com/app/en/articles/118091-payments-costs-in-the-spond-app); [Spond help centre](https://www.spond.com/help-centre/)
- UK fee 2.5% + 20p per transaction (as of July 2026, per third parties), group covers fee by default, fee charged per product in a request — [SportMember vs Spond](https://www.sportmember.co.uk/en/clubmanagement/sportmember-vs-spond) (competitor); [ClubPal Spond cost](https://www.clubpal.app/compare/spond-alternative) (competitor); [ClubZap vs Spond](https://clubzap.com/gb/blog/clubzap-vs-spond-which-is-right-for-your-sports-club/) (competitor)
- Superdraw fundraising: club paid 65% of sales (Spond retains 35%) — [Spond fundraising terms](https://www.spond.com/en-us/fundraising-terms-stb/); [Spond Superdraw FAQ](https://help.spond.com/app/en/articles/25733-spond-superdraw-faqs-for-admins)
- Brand partnerships fund keeping the app free — [Spond brand partnerships](https://www.spond.com/news-and-blog/spond-brand-partnerships/)
- Spond Club website: 6-month free trial then reported £19/month (+VAT per another source); BeeNet says no published self-serve price for multi-team layer — [Spond club website](https://www.spond.com/news-and-blog/club-website-spond/); [BeeNet Spond alternatives](https://beenet.app/blog/spond-alternatives-for-sports-clubs-2026/) (competitor)
- Feature gaps: competitor comparisons say Spond lacks player performance stats and AI match reports; logs final score and Player of the Match vote only; fines tracking examples come from TeamStats/SportMember not Spond — [TeamStats vs Spond](https://www.teamstats.net/football-coaching/apps/teamstats-vs-spond); [Mingle Sport Spond alternative](https://mingle.sport/spond-alternative/); [Spond vs TeamStats (Spond blog)](https://www.spond.com/news-and-blog/spond-vs-teamstats/)
- UK users: "over 1.5 million users in the UK" (latest Spond post); 1m UK MAU passed Nov 2023; ~4m MAU globally — [Spond grassroots football guide](https://www.spond.com/news-and-blog/how-to-manage-grassroots-football-clubs/); [South East Online Nov 2023](https://southeastonline.co.uk/2023/11/21/spond-hits-1-million-monthly-active-users-in-the-uk/); [Spond brand partnerships](https://www.spond.com/news-and-blog/spond-brand-partnerships/)
- Grassroots partnership with BBFA (Berks & Bucks FA) — [Spond BBFA partnership](https://www.spond.com/news-and-blog/spond-grassroots-football-partnership/)
- Sentiment: Trustpilot reported as 2.3/5 (58 reviews) or 2.9 by different blogs; complaints: notification overload, no bulk accept/decline, payouts held 10–15 days, slow generic support, web version breaking; praise: "condense 25 hours of admin down to 4 hours" — [Trustpilot UK Spond](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/spond.com); [Tactico comparison](https://tacticosport.com/news/best-football-team-management-apps-2026); [BeeNet](https://beenet.app/blog/football-club-management-software/); [Lewes Lenny "To Spond or Not to Spond"](https://leweslenny.substack.com/p/to-spond-or-not-to-spond)
- Commonly paired: "Spond for the club, Squadd for the football" — [Squadd blog](https://www.squadd.co.uk/blog/best-football-team-management-apps)

### Inferences
- Spond owns availability/payments; a PWA should integrate with or complement it (match-day content, stats, fines, supporter view, graphics) rather than replace it.
- For a £10 match sub, Spond's fee is 45p (4.5%) — a meaningful cost on small grassroots payments.

### Gaps
- Spond's own UK pricing page not fetched; fee confirmed only via third parties plus Spond's model description.
- No official confirmation of absence of fines feature.

## Other competitors: TeamApp, Heja, Teamo, ClubBuzz, Clubify, Playwaze, Sportlomo, Teamer, SportMember, TeamStats, ClubZap, FA Matchday, Kitman

### Takeaway
The market splits into (a) free ad-supported team organisers (TeamApp, Heja, Teamer-now-closed, SportMember Basic, TeamStats free), monetised by ads + payment fees + cheap premium; (b) club platforms/websites charging ~£19–£99/month or quote-based (Pitchero, ClubBuzz £299/yr, SportMember PRO ≥£22/month, ClubZap, Playwaze); (c) the FA's own free Matchday app tied to Full-Time. Only TeamStats and SportMember clearly offer fines + player stats; only TeamStats offers auto (AI) match reports; social graphics are a separate niche market (Terrace, TRYMF etc.).

### Cited Findings
**Teamer (CLOSED)**
- Closed Monday 5 October 2026; users emailed 5 Aug 2026; Premium subs cancelled in Sept; owner Pitchero Group — [Teamer Shutdown FAQs](https://help.teamer.net/knowledge/teamer-shutdown-faqs); [ClubZap on Teamer shutdown](https://clubzap.com/gb/blog/teamer-is-shutting-down-what-it-means-for-your-club-and-what-to-do-next/)

# Matchday graphics, video/analytics tools and the FA's own digital tools (grassroots England, 2025-2026)

Research date: 19 Nov 2026. Method note: web searches only. Direct page fetches of thefa.com, grassrootstechnology.thefa.com and uk.shop.veo.co failed (those hosts are blocked in this session's network), so FA and Veo page content below comes from search-result snippets and was not read in full. Treat quoted wording as indicative and re-check primary pages before relying on it.

## (a) Matchday graphics products: who sells them, at what price, and do they auto-pull fixtures/results?

### Takeaway
The market is fragmented: a few grassroots club apps bundle "one-tap" matchday graphics (Clubbie is the clearest example, with a freemium plan at about $7-13/month or $60-70/year depending on storefront), while most clubs use Canva or one-off template packs ($40-type bundles). I found no product that verifiably pulls fixtures and results automatically from FA Full-Time to make graphics. The FA itself gives no feed that would make this easy (see section c).

### Cited Findings
- **Clubbie** (grassroots club app) advertises "ONE-TAP SOCIALS: Instantly generate professional-quality Matchday Graphics" covering lineups, results and player of the match — [App Store (US)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/clubbie/id1574344841); [mwm.ai listing](https://mwm.ai/apps/clubbie/1574344841)
- Clubbie+ in-app prices vary by storefront: US $6.99/month or $59.99/year; Slovenia €7.99/month or €69.99/year; New Zealand NZ$12.99/month or NZ$69.99/year. No GBP price was found — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/clubbie/id1574344841); [App Store SI](https://apps.apple.com/si/app/clubbie/id1574344841); [App Store NZ](https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/clubbie/id1574344841)
- One Clubbie storefront page says the app is free for the whole 2026/27 season, with no trial and no card needed. This is unverified and conflicts with the paid Clubbie+ tiers listed elsewhere — [App Store (UY)](https://apps.apple.com/uy/app/clubbie-grassroots-sport/id1574344841)
- **4KMATCH: PRO Football Designs**: a freemium iOS/Android app for grassroots and academy players that claims a database of 4,500+ teams. The source is an aggregator that rates its own confidence as low — [marlvel.ai](https://marlvel.ai/api/llm/apps/graphics-design/4kmatch-pro-football-designs)
- **Social Sports Manager**: an App Store app whose results graphics need the score entered by hand, so nothing is pulled automatically — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1220504256)
- **SFL Soccer**: free with in-app purchases, and includes a "create soccer graphics for your club" feature — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1581569240)
- **FootballGPT "Graphics Studio"** creates matchday result cards for social media. No price was visible — [FootballGPT](https://footballgpt.co/features/graphics-studio)
- **Krumzi** offers game-day graphics in team colours for sports clubs. No price was found — [Krumzi](https://www.krumzi.com/for/organizations/sports-clubs)
- **Canva** has a public library of football templates (free/Pro), which is the de facto DIY tool — [Canva football templates](https://www.canva.com/templates/s/football/)
- One-off template packs are common on Payhip and Gumroad, e.g. a 100+ Canva football template kit at about $40 — [Payhip example](https://payhip.com/b/5iAeh); [Payhip GraphicLabDesign](https://payhip.com/GraphicLabDesign); [Gumroad Arena Graphics](https://sebugwawo.gumroad.com)
- Some grassroots clubs (e.g. Legends FC) distribute Canva templates to members — [legendsfc.net/canva](https://www.legendsfc.net/canva)
- Cheshire FA published a club marketing package that includes Starting Lineup, Goal update, Match update and Man of the Match templates (a free county-FA resource; no price) — [Cheshire FA PDF](https://thefa.com/-/media/cfa/cheshirefa/files/club-marketing-package-in-full.ashx)
- US-centric automated graphics services are much more expensive: Boxout Sports Premium is $275/month or $2,400/year — [Boxout pricing](https://boxoutsports.com/pricing). Envato Elements, a general creative subscription with football carousel templates, starts at about $16.50/month — [Envato](https://elements.envato.com/football-match-social-media-instagram-carousel-RQ49XA2)
- Freelance option: Fiverr gigs for sports-team social templates — [Fiverr](https://fiverr.com/marlo_neikes/create-a-sports-team-content-template)
- A startup-idea site lists "Auto-generated graphics for youth sports leagues" as an unmet idea, a weak signal that automated league-fed graphics are seen as a gap — [appideas.ai](https://appideas.ai/ideas/league-gameday-graphics/)

### Inferences
- The named products in the brief ("Matchday Graphics", "Footy Graphics", "ClubGraphics", "Sportography", "PlayPass", "Matchday Pro", "Pitchside", "Teamsheet") did not appear in searches as established UK products with public pricing. They may be small, renamed or nonexistent; I could not verify them.
- Price anchor for a club graphics tool: about £5-10/month or £50-70/year (a Clubbie+-like freemium), versus £0 for Canva free plus volunteer time. UK grassroots clubs are unlikely to pay Boxout-level prices.
- Auto-pulling fixtures and results from Full-Time is a real differentiator because nobody visibly offers it. The obstacle is that Full-Time has no public API (section c), so any such product would rely on manual entry, an admin's code snippet, or scraping, which carries terms risk.

### Gaps
- No UK (GBP) prices found for Clubbie+, Krumzi, FootballGPT or 4KMATCH.
- Could not confirm whether Pitchero offers graphics generation, or at what price.
- No independent reviews or usage numbers for any graphics app.

## (b) Video/analytics products for grassroots (Veo, Hudl, Pixellot, Trace): costs and willingness to pay

### Takeaway
Veo is the most visible grassroots option in England. The camera is about £999 (often £200 off through county FA partnerships), plus a mandatory subscription from about £360-499/year (Starter) up to about £39-142/month by tier. Hudl and Pixellot do not publish GBP prices (Hudl's US list prices are $400-1,600 per team per year). County FA partnership deals with Veo point to real, if still minority, willingness to pay among clubs, mainly larger youth clubs.

### Cited Findings
- **Veo UK shop:** the Veo Cam costs £999, and a subscription is required at £39-£142/month depending on team size, one subscription per camera. The page is undated — [Veo UK shop](https://uk.shop.veo.co/) (search snippet; fetch blocked)
- **Veo Starter:** an East Riding FA page says the Starter subscription lets grassroots clubs film up to 5 games a month for £360/year — [East Riding FA](https://www.eastridingfa.com/clubs-and-facilities/veo). A more recent UK parent site says Starter is from £499/year on a 12-month minimum term — [footballparent.co.uk](https://www.footballparent.co.uk/football-gear/veo-camera-alternatives). These **conflict**; £499 is probably the current figure.
- Veo's official pricing page lists Starter, Team, Club, Enterprise and Veo Go. Starter and Team allow 1 team per camera and Club allows 10 teams per camera; prices did not render in the snippet — [veo.com/pricing](https://www.veo.com/pricing)
- Third-party USD figure: Club is $239/month, or from $134/month billed annually — [aitoolscoop](https://aitoolscoop.com/tool/veo/) (unverified aggregator)
- **Veo Go** (iPhone-based, no camera hardware): 1 team, 15 users, 20 recording hours. No live streaming, analytics or Player Spotlight. No GBP price was found — [Veo support](https://support.veo.com/hc/en-us/articles/33196190545297-Veo-Go-plan-explained-Features-and-limitations); [Veo Go](https://veo.co/product/veo-go)
- **County FA partnerships with Veo** (£200 off the camera with a 1-2 year Team/Club/Enterprise subscription): Suffolk FA, Berks & Bucks FA, Sheffield & Hallamshire FA (SYFA page), East Riding FA, Shropshire FA (2023), and the FAW in Wales. Some offers ran to 29 Jan 2026 or 31 Mar 2026 — [Suffolk FA x Veo](https://www.veo.co/partnership/suffolk-fa); [Berks & Bucks FA x Veo](https://www.veo.com/partnership/berks-bucksfa); [SYFA x Veo](https://www.veo.co/partnership/syfa); [Shropshire FA](https://www.shropshirefa.com/news/2023/mar/14/veo); [FAW x Veo](https://www.veo.co/partnership/the-faw)
- An Irish amateur ("typical Sunday League") business league bought Veo cameras to record and stream every game — [Veo case study](https://www.veo.co/article/how-live-streaming-is-transforming-cork-business-league) (vendor source)
- **Hudl Focus Flex** (portable camera): annual subscription, priced per club, with UK pricing only from sales — [Hudl Focus Flex FAQ (en_gb)](https://www.hudl.com/en_gb/products/focus/faq/flex)
- **Hudl club soccer** (US list): Bronze from $400 per team per year, up to about $1,600 — [Hudl pricing](https://www.hudl.com/pricing/club/hudl/soccer). **Hudl Assist soccer** (human-tagged stats): $700-$1,600 per team per season — [Hudl Assist pricing](https://www.hudl.com/pricing/assist/soccer). The UK non-league page lists no prices — [Hudl UK non-league](https://www.hudl.com/en_gb/sports/football/non-league)
- Hudl runs a "Backed by Hudl" competition for UK grassroots clubs (e.g. Oxhey FC) — [Hudl blog](https://www.hudl.com/blog/backed-by-hudl-winner-oxhey-fc)
- **Pixellot Air**: launched 2021 at "from $849" in the US plus a monthly plan, with pricing varying by territory. No current UK price — [Hudl/Pixellot search via SVG](https://www.sportsvideo.org/2021/03/04/fc-barcelonas-barca-innovation-hub-leverages-pixellot-air-to-dissect-training-sessions/)
- Pixellot partnered with Pitchero (2021) to bring video to UK clubs, and Pitchero has a Pixellot landing page — [SVG Europe](https://www.svgeurope.org/blog/headlines/pixellot-to-help-pitchero-bring-video-to-its-online-platform-that-serves-uk-clubs-and-teams/); [join.pitchero.com/pixellot](https://join.pitchero.com/pixellot). Pixellot also signed with Our Game (2023) — [Advanced Television](https://www.advanced-television.com/2023/07/19/pixellot-partners-with-our-game/)
- A rugby-focused UK guide puts budgeting at roughly £1,000 for hardware plus a subscription from several hundred pounds a year up to about £1,700 — [Framesports](https://framesports.ai/blog/how-much-does-rugby-video-analysis-cost/)
- **Trace**: no UK price found. A Canadian listing shows conflicting figures ($650 vs $390), and competitor pages claim Trace needs multiple subscriptions (treat as biased) — [Veo vs Trace (Veo-authored)](https://www.veo.com/veo-vs-trace); [XbotGo on Trace](https://xbotgo.com/blogs/buying-guide/traceup-alternatives); [Trace's own comparison](https://traceup.com/compare/trace-vs-veo)
- Budget context: third-party estimates put English grassroots player fees at £80-150 per season and team season costs at £2,000-5,000+ — [TeamStats](https://teamstats.net/blog/the-rising-cost-of-grassroots-football) (vendor blog)

### Inferences
- A Veo setup costs about £1,000 up front plus roughly £360-1,700 a year, which is a large share of a small adult Saturday or Sunday club's £2-5k season budget. Adoption is likely concentrated in larger youth clubs and higher-step non-league sides. County FA discount schemes show the FA network sees demand.
- Clubs that pay for video show some willingness to pay for "content", but that spend is on video. A cheap text and graphics product would sit in a much lower price band (pounds a month, not hundreds).

### Gaps
- No verified current GBP prices for Veo Team/Club, Veo Go, Hudl, Pixellot or Trace.
- No data on how many English grassroots clubs own Veo/Hudl/Pixellot. No Reddit or forum threads surfaced.

## (c) The FA's free tools: Full-Time, Matchday app, Whole Game System, FA Player. Data exposed, API/feeds/embeds, and terms on reuse

### Takeaway
FA Full-Time is the official public record of league fixtures, results and tables. It has **no public API, RSS or iCal feed** that I could find. The only sanctioned reuse route is "Code Snippets" (embed widgets for table, recent results, upcoming fixtures and team fixtures/results), and these were **removed from public pages and are now available only to logged-in team/league admins**. The FA's general website terms prohibit extracting or reproducing content without permission, and FA app terms explicitly ban scraping and text/data mining (including for AI). Despite this, third-party scrapers of Full-Time exist (Apify). The FA Matchday app, which sits on top of Whole Game System, handles team admin and result submission. The FA Player is a streaming app for women's elite football, not grassroots.

### Cited Findings
**Full-Time embeds and feeds**
- The FA's Grassroots Technology help article says: "For security purposes full time feeds / code snippets have been removed from public pages." Snippets are available to registered team admins, which can include website developers. Path: Full-Time admin, then Media, then Code Snippets. Options: Table, Recent Results, Upcoming Fixtures, Team Fixtures/Results. Club fixtures/results work only within one league, not across leagues — [FA Grassroots Technology: Embedding league tables, fixtures & results (Full-Time feeds)](https://grassrootstechnology.thefa.com/support/solutions/articles/48001158072-embedding-league-tables-fixtures-tables-full-time-feeds-) (search snippet; undated; fetch blocked)
- A request on the FA's Grassroots Technology forum for Full-Time to generate .ics calendar files was marked as not taken forward, because "the same work is being done under Matchday enhancements" — [FA Grassroots Technology forum](https://grassrootstechnology.thefa.com/support/discussions/topics/48000563653) (undated)
- No official public API, RSS or iCal for Full-Time was found in any search.
- Third-party scrapers exist: Apify "FA Full Time League table scraper", "FA Full Time Results scraper" and "THEFA Leagues and Teams Data Scraper" — [Apify league table](https://apify.com/abe/fa-full-time-league-table-scraper); [Apify results](https://apify.com/abe/fa-full-time-results-scraper/api); [Apify leagues/teams](https://apify.com/autosoldier/thefa-leagues-and-teams-data-scraper). A WordPress "Club Stats" plugin also appears in Full-Time-related results — [WordPress plugin](https://fair.pm/packages/plugins/club-stats)

**Terms of use**
- The FA's general terms say users may not "publish, distribute, extract, re-utilise, or reproduce any part of the Media in any form" except as permitted by law or by the rights holder — [thefa.com/public/terms](https://www.thefa.com/public/terms) (snippet). **Unverified** whether these terms formally cover fulltime.thefa.com; I could not find Full-Time-specific terms.
- The FA app terms (England fan app help centre) explicitly prohibit text or data mining and web scraping, including to develop, train or validate AI systems, to the extent permitted by law — [The FA App terms of service](https://englandfanapp.thefa.com/support/solutions/articles/48001281400-the-fa-app-terms-of-service). These apply to the FA app, not Full-Time; they show the FA's stance.
- WGS portal privacy notice exists, relevant to personal data — [thefa.com/public/privacy/wgs-portal](https://thefa.com/public/privacy/wgs-portal)

**Third-party integrations**
- Pitchero's help centre says Pitchero can do a **one-time export of fixtures per division**, usually at the start of the season, for "NGB competition engines: The FA Full-Time". Live daily sync is documented for ECB Play-Cricket, not Full-Time. Leagues can also have fixtures imported into Pitchero's Competitions Database on request — [Pitchero: 3rd-party powered competitions](https://help.pitchero.com/knowledge/3rd-party-powered-competitions); [Pitchero: league competitions](https://help.pitchero.com/knowledge/competitions)
- Pitchero's starting price on Capterra is from £12 (third-party listing, free version available) — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/148390/Pitchero/)
- No evidence was found of an FA-licensed live Full-Time data feed to any third party.

**FA Matchday app**
- Launched 2018, integrated with Whole Game System and Full-Time. Features: send live scores and match reports to league administration, submit results from the touchline straight after full time (to Full-Time), "Match View" to follow live updates, eligible-player lists and discipline data from WGS, availability and game reminders, team sheets — [Lancashire FA](https://lancashirefa.com/news/2018/jun/14/fa-set-to-launch-new-matchday-app); [Wiltshire FA](https://www.wiltshirefa.com/news/2018/jun/13/matchday-is-coming); [Northumberland FA](https://www.northumberlandfa.com/news/2018/jun/11/matchday-is-coming); [Shropshire FA](https://www.shropshirefa.com/news/2018/jun/15/fa-set-to-launch-new-matchday-app)
- Payments: a 2019 PayPal partnership for match fees, subs and fines through the app — [Surrey FA](https://www.surreyfa.com/news/2019/feb/27/the-football-association-team-up-with-paypal); [Gloucestershire FA](https://gloucestershirefa.com/news/2020/mar/09/matchday-app-subscriptions-and-payments)
- FA case studies of grassroots clubs using Matchday — [FA news 2019](https://www.thefa.com/news/2019/nov/07/matchday-app-071119); [Abbey Meads FC 2020](https://www.thefa.com/news/2020/jul/28/abbey-meads-fc-matchday-app-280720)
- A "Matchday 9.0.0" release note describes backend modernisation (undated) — [FA Grassroots Technology](https://grassrootstechnology.thefa.com/support/discussions/topics/48000565067)
- A 2026 third-party guide describes Full-Time as the official record for league results and the Matchday app as covering fixtures, results and player registration through WGS — [Sportplan 2026](https://www.sportplan.net/drills/Football/guides/best-free-grassroots-football-apps-2026.jsp); [Sportplan: FA Matchday and Spond alternatives](https://www.sportplan.net/drills/Football/guides/fa-matchday-spond-alternatives-football.jsp)
- Rival apps market against Matchday (e.g. TeamStats comparison pages) — [TeamStats vs MatchDay](https://www.teamstats.net/football-coaching/apps/teamstats-vs-matchday) (vendor)

**Whole Game System**
- WGS is the FA's registration and admin backbone (player registration, affiliation, discipline), feeding Matchday and Full-Time — [FA WGS benefits](https://www.thefa.com/get-involved/matchday/whole-game-system/benefits)

**FA Player**
- A free streaming app/site for women's football (WSL, Women's Championship, Lionesses, Women's FA Cup, League Cup). It does not stream grassroots matches — [FA Player FAQs](https://www.thefa.com/womens-girls-football/leagues-and-competitions/the-fa-player/faqs); [App Store (GB)](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/the-fa-player/id1475496828); [Norfolk FA launch 2019](https://norfolkfa.com/news/2019/sep/16/introducing-the-fa-player)

### Inferences
- The Matchday app and Full-Time are admin and record-keeping tools. Neither produces fan-facing content: no graphics, no narrative match reports, no social sharing assets, and Full-Time has no public feed. That leaves clubs re-typing scores into Canva or social posts. This is the gap.
- A product that reuses Full-Time data should treat scraping as legally risky. Safer routes are: (1) club admins paste their own Full-Time code snippet, or enter results once in the product; (2) the product ingests data the club already owns (its own scores and scorers); (3) the product seeks a data agreement with the FA or county FA. Facts such as scores are not strongly protected by copyright, but UK database right and contract terms may apply. This is an inference, not legal advice.
- The FA steering calendar (.ics) work into Matchday suggests its roadmap focuses on team admin, not public data access.

### Gaps
- Could not read Full-Time's own terms page or the full Code Snippets article (host blocked). The date of the snippet removal is unknown.
- Could not confirm whether the FA Matchday app is still fully supported in 2026-27, or whether an "FA Grassroots" app has replaced it. No source found saying it was retired.
- No official FA statement found on licensing Full-Time data to commercial third parties.

## (d) Automated or AI-written match reports for grassroots clubs

### Takeaway
A few vendors already offer AI or template match reports from manually entered data: TeamStats, FootballGPT (voice notes to report), and Framesports (rugby-focused, from video analysis). None appears to generate reports automatically from FA Full-Time data. Pricing is mostly undisclosed.

### Cited Findings
- **TeamStats** match report generator: takes goals, line-ups, subs, cards and ratings entered by the coach and "instantly turns that data into a coherent, polished report", editable before sharing — [TeamStats blog](https://www.teamstats.net/blog/generating-match-reports-automatically); [TeamStats: match reports in seconds](https://www.teamstats.net/blog/how-to-write-football-match-reports-in-seconds-no-templates-needed)
- **FootballGPT**: record post-match voice notes to get an AI match report with player ratings, timeline, tactical observations and training recommendations — [FootballGPT voice match notes](https://footballgpt.co/features/voice-match-notes); [FootballGPT writing tools](https://footballgpt.co/features/writing-tools)
- **Framesports**: auto-generates a written match report article with AI after video analysis (rugby-focused) — [Framesports docs](https://docs.framesports.ai/social-media/articles)
- **OneFoundation**: a grassroots/academy match analysis tool at £12.99/month (Gumroad listing). Unclear whether it writes narrative reports — [Gumroad](https://studyo13.gumroad.com/l/wjqrkw)
- Enterprise auto-sports-writing (Retresco, Texta) targets media and newsrooms, not clubs — [Retresco](https://retresco.de/en/automated-sports-content); [Texta](https://texta.ai/ai-writing-assistant/media-and-communications/sports-writer)

### Inferences
- AI match reports are emerging but bolted onto team-management apps. A product combining "score in, then report plus graphics plus social posts out" for adult/Sunday league clubs appears thinly served.

### Gaps
- No pricing for TeamStats' or FootballGPT's report features. No adoption data. No UK-specific independent reviews.

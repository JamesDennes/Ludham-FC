# Grassroots football in England: market size, club budgets, sponsorship and willingness to pay

Method note: research done 19 Nov 2026. WebFetch could not reach thefa.com, forza.com, networldsports.co.uk or lordslibrary.parliament.uk because those hosts are blocked in this session's network settings. Figures from those pages come from search-engine summaries of them, not full-text reads, so check the key numbers against the original PDFs before publishing. ## 1. How many affiliated clubs and teams there are, and how they split by segment and trend

### Takeaway
England has about 124,500 affiliated teams and 1.6 million affiliated players (FA, Sept 2026). Team numbers are up about 10% since the end of 2023-24, and female teams are growing fastest. No current official national count of clubs turned up. Using London FA's ratio of about 5 teams per club, there are probably about 20,000-30,000 clubs. That is an estimate. About 18,000 clubs are FA-accredited.

### Cited Findings
- **Teams (latest):** more than 124,500 affiliated teams, up 10% over the past two seasons (since the end of 2023-24). Affiliated players rose 4% to 1.6 million. — [FA, "A progress update on our Grassroots Strategy", 28 Sep 2026](https://www.thefa.com/news/2026/sep/28/a-thriving-grassroots-game-update-20262809); [FA Grassroots Mid-Strategy Review 2026](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/thefacom-new/files/documents/2026/grassroots-mid-strategy-review.ashx)
- **Teams (baseline):** the FA Grassroots Strategy 2024-28 cites "114k teams across England", a football workforce of over 1 million and 21,000 facilities, together contributing £15.9bn to society (2024 baseline). — [FA Grassroots Strategy 2024-28 (North Riding FA copy)](https://thefa.com/-/media/cfa/northridingfa/files/governance/2024-25/the-fa-grassroots-strategy-2024-28.ashx?la=en)
- **Female players:** about 255,000 female affiliated players within the 1.6 million total. — [FA Grassroots Mid-Strategy Review 2026](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/thefacom-new/files/documents/2026/grassroots-mid-strategy-review.ashx)
- **Female teams:** up 14.3% between 2023 and 2025. — [FA 2024-25 Annual Report (published Apr 2026)](https://www.thefa.com/news/2026/apr/02/2024-25-fa-annual-report)
- **Women and girls, longer trend:** women and girls playing up 56% over the four years of the Inspiring Positive Change strategy (2020-24). — [FA, Sep 2024](https://www.thefa.com/news/2024/sep/27/women-and-girls-stratgy-progress-inspiring-positive-change-20242709). Female youth teams up 39% between Oct 2021 and Dec 2023. — [FA, Dec 2023](https://www.thefa.com/news/2023/dec/20/inspiring-positive-change-update-20232012)
- **Clubs:** a House of Lords Library briefing (late 2025) refers to a "crucial network" of around 18,000 FA-accredited clubs. Accreditation covers only part of all affiliated clubs, and the briefing's own date and source could not be confirmed. — [House of Lords Library, "Non-league and grassroots football: What is the state of play?"](https://lordslibrary.parliament.uk/non-league-and-grassroots-football-what-is-the-state-of-play/)
- **Clubs-to-teams ratio (one county):** London FA affiliated "just over 1,000 clubs" and passed 5,000 teams in 2023-24, about 5 teams per club. — [London FA AGM 2024 CEO report](https://thefa.com/-/media/cfa/londonfa/files/agm/2024-agm/lfa-agm-2024---ceo-report.ashx)
- **County scale example:** Surrey FA reported 54,261 male players and 687 disability players affiliated in 2024-25. — [Surrey County FA accounts 2025](https://opencharities.uk/ew/1169077/files/2025-06-30-accounts.html)
- **Wider participation:** over 15 million people in England play football regularly, supported by about 1 million volunteers. — [FA Council Annual Report 2024-25](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/thefacom-new/files/about-the-fa/2025/fa-council-annual-report-2024-25.ashx?la=en)
- **National League System (top of the adult Saturday grassroots pyramid):**
  - Step 5 has 16 divisions and Step 6 has 17 divisions in both 2025-26 and 2026-27.
  - Regulatory maximums are 320 clubs at Step 5 and 340 at Step 6, so about 660 clubs at Steps 5-6 combined.
  - Sources: [FA NLS Regulations 2025-26](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/files/thefaportal/governance-docs/rules-of-the-association/2025-26/21---national-league-system-regulations.ashx); [FA NLS Regulations 2026-27](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/files/thefaportal/governance-docs/rules-of-the-association/2026-27/section-21---national-league-system-regulations.ashx)
- **Step 7 no longer exists in the NLS:** it was abolished before 2020-21 and replaced by feeder leagues overseen by County FAs. Many sources still use "step 7" informally for these feeder leagues. — [Wikipedia: National League System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_System); [Non League Insider](https://www.nonleagueinsider.com/what-is-non-league-football/step-7/)
- **Sunday league and adult 11-a-side decline (all evidence is older):**
  - An FA-commissioned study found 2,360 grassroots teams folded between 2010 and 2015. Over the same period, regular 11-a-side players aged 16+ fell by about 180,000 since 2005 (reported 2018). — [Telegraph via PressReader, 2018](https://www.pressreader.com/uk/the-daily-telegraph-sport/20180924/281964608640104)
  - An FA release from Jan 2015 said adult male affiliated 11-a-side team numbers fell over five seasons, while average squads got bigger. — [FA, Jan 2015](https://www.thefa.com/news/2015/jan/29/football-participation-on-rise)
  - Local examples of Sunday leagues shrinking or folding: [Grassroots Post 2022](https://www.grassrootspost.uk/2022/03/28/mens-sunday-league/); [Yorkshire Post (league folded with 3 teams)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/news/people/sunday-football-league-in-yorkshire-established-in-1971-folds-after-dwindling-to-just-three-teams-3719881); Chichester & West Sussex Sunday league fell from about 60 teams in the mid-1990s to 6 teams by 2017-18 ([Bognor Regis Observer](https://www.bognor.co.uk/sport/football/chichester-and-sunday-league-disbanded-after-half-a-century-830894)).
- **Small-sided and veterans:** County FAs charge separate affiliation categories for "Adult Small-Sided / Veterans / Disability" teams, for example Lincolnshire FA 2025-26. This shows these teams are affiliated as their own segment, but no national count was found. — [Lincolnshire FA affiliation pack 2025](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/cfa/lincolnshirefa/files/affiliation-pack-2025.ashx?la=en)
- **Disability and adult women's teams (undated):** nearly 200 affiliated female disability teams, and over 1,800 adult women's teams playing weekly competitive football. — [Vercida, Women's and Girls' football at The FA](https://www.vercida.com/uk/features/women-s-and-girls-football-at-the-fa)

### Inferences
- **Club count (estimate):** applying London's ratio of about 5 teams per club to 124,500 teams gives about 25,000 clubs in England. A reasonable range is 20,000-30,000. Youth clubs often run 10-30+ teams, while adult Sunday clubs are often single-team, so the ratio varies a lot.
- **Segment mix (estimate):** youth and mini-soccer teams are very likely the majority of the 124,500 teams. That is consistent with the girls' growth, the large youth club structures, and adult 11-a-side decline. No sourced split was found.
- **NLS steps 5-6 are a tiny slice:** about 660 clubs, or under 3% of all clubs. Most adult Saturday clubs sit in county and feeder leagues (old "step 7" and below).
- **Trend:** total teams are growing, driven by female and youth teams. Adult male Sunday 11-a-side has long been in decline. The figures above are 2015-2018 era, and the trend is reported as continuing locally.

### Gaps
- No official 2024-26 national count of affiliated clubs or leagues, because the thefa.com full PDFs could not be fetched. Check the FA 2024-25 Annual Report and the Mid-Strategy Review PDF directly.
- No official split of teams into adult male Saturday, Sunday, youth, mini-soccer, women's, girls' and small-sided/veterans. The FA may hold this in Whole Game System data; ask the FA research team or County FA AGM reports.
- No current (2023-26) national figure on the Sunday league decline.
- Sport England Active Lives football figures were not retrieved.

## 2. How many leagues and County FAs there are, and how fixtures are administered (FA Full-Time)

### Takeaway
50 County FAs cover England (including the Amateur FA, the Armed Forces FA and others). FA Full-Time is the default league administration platform, used by well over 800-1,000 leagues for fixtures, results and tables. Full-Time is integrated with the Whole Game System for player registration.

### Cited Findings
- The FA works with a network of 50 County FAs. — [FA 2024-25 Annual Report](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/thefacom-new/files/about-the-fa/2026/2024-25-fa-annual-report.ashx?la=en)
- The Grassroots Code of Governance becomes mandatory for County FAs from 2025-26. 19 County FAs were fully compliant in 2023-24, and nearly all were by 2024-25. — [FA 2024-25 Annual Report](https://www.thefa.com/news/2026/apr/02/2024-25-fa-annual-report); [FA 2023-24 Annual Report](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/thefacom-new/files/about-the-fa/2025/fa-annual-report-statement-2023-24-low-res.ashx?la=en)
- **Full-Time league counts (sources disagree):**
  - West Riding FA refers to a list of leagues "(over 1000) already using Full-Time". — [West Riding FA, Full-Time](https://www.westridingfa.com/leagues/full-time)
  - A third-party data scraper describes Full-Time as powering "800+" English grassroots and youth leagues, and notes there is no public API. — [Apify actor README](https://apify.com/parviz_a/my-actor-2.md)
- **What Full-Time does:** league admins set up leagues and fixtures and enter results. It generates public fixtures, results, tables and stats pages, or feeds a league's existing website. It has been linked to the Whole Game System since 2016. — [Kent FA online club administration](https://www.kentfa.com/leagues-and-clubs/club-management/online-club-administration); [Suffolk FA Full-Time launch](https://www.suffolkfa.com/news/2017/oct/31/full-time-service-launch)
- The FA reinvested £158m into the game in 2024-25. — [FA 2024-25 Annual Report](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/thefacom-new/files/about-the-fa/2026/2024-25-fa-annual-report.ashx?la=en)

### Inferences
- Fixtures and results are effectively a free FA monopoly through Full-Time. A paid club app would have to work alongside it, for example by importing Full-Time fixtures, rather than replace it. The lack of a public API is a technical constraint: integrations rely on scraping or manual entry.
- Full-Time is used by more than 1,000 leagues (in many of which each age-group league is a separate entity). The figure is consistent with a market of tens of thousands of clubs.

### Gaps
- No authoritative current total of leagues sanctioned in England.
- No count of clubs or teams actively using Full-Time.

## 3. Typical grassroots club budgets: income, spending, subs and fees

### Takeaway
No national average budget surveys turned up. Guides and club examples put a typical grassroots team's running costs at a few thousand pounds a season, and one guide's example puts a small club at about £8,000 a season before trophies. Spending is dominated by pitch hire (£1,800-£3,600 a season in that example) and referees (£20-£35 per youth game, £40-£50 per adult game). County FA affiliation is small: about £10-£40 per team, plus about £70 per club for public liability. Clubs fund this from subs, match fees and sponsorship. 99% of clubs report rising costs, and 57% say their funding is insufficient (FORZA 2026).

### Cited Findings
- **Overall budget (one example):** "a grassroots football club can spend around £8,000 a season before it wins a single trophy", of which pitch hire is £1,800-£3,600. — [Avec Sport blog](https://avecsport.com/blog/what-are-the-costs-of-running-a-football-club) (vendor blog, indicative only)
- **Referee fees:** £20-£35 per youth match and £40-£50 per adult match, roughly £700-£1,750 per team per season. — [Avec Sport blog](https://avecsport.com/blog/what-are-the-costs-of-running-a-football-club) (vendor blog)
- **Youth subs:** about £80-£200 per child per year in annual subs, or around £3-£5 per week. — [Sportplan guide, Football subs and match fees](https://www.sportplan.net/drills/Football/guides/football-subs-match-fees.jsp)
- **How fees are usually split:** fixed costs (affiliation, league fees, kit) go into annual or termly subs, and variable costs (pitch hire, referees) into a per-player match fee. — [Sportplan guide](https://www.sportplan.net/drills/Football/guides/football-subs-match-fees.jsp)
- **Affiliation fees by County FA:**

  | County FA | Season | Adult team | Youth team | Public liability | Personal accident |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | [Lincolnshire FA](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/cfa/lincolnshirefa/files/affiliation-pack-2025.ashx?la=en) | 2025-26 | £38 (11v11) | £21 | £70 per club | £62 per adult team, £25 per youth team |
  | [Hertfordshire FA](https://www.hertfordshirefa.com/news/2024/may/21/club-affiliation-2024-25) | 2024-25 | £30 (non-NLS) | £10 | £70 per club | £35 per adult team, £20 per youth team |
  | [Liverpool FA](https://www.liverpoolfa.com/leagues-and-clubs/club-management/affiliation) | 2024-25 | £30 | £10 | not given | not given |
  | [Sheffield & Hallamshire FA](https://www.sheffieldfa.com/leagues-and-clubs/club-affiliation) | frozen since 2022-23 | £36.50 | £26 | not given | not given |

  - Lincolnshire's £21 rate also covers adult small-sided, veterans and disability teams.
  - Liverpool notes that affiliation switched from a per-club fee to a per-team fee for all County FAs in 2024-25.
- **Rising costs:**
  - FORZA 2026 survey (409 UK clubs, July 2026): 99% of clubs saw costs rise in the past year, nearly three-quarters "significantly", and 57% say funding is insufficient. — [FORZA 2026 State of Grassroots Football Report](https://www.forza.com/uk/football/grassroots-report)
  - FORZA 2025 survey: 1,001 UK club responses (July 2025); 81% cite lack of funding or sponsorship as their biggest challenge, as cited by a third party. — [FORZA 2025 report](https://www.networldsports.co.uk/football/grassroots-report); [Kit Funder summary](https://kitfunder.ai/blog-posts/grassroots-sports-sponsorship-made-easy)
  - FORZA's report sponsor is an equipment maker; treat these as indicative.
- **Income sources:** in FORZA 2024 (180+ clubs at step 6 and below), sponsorship (74%) and membership fees (54%) were the primary revenue sources. — [FORZA Grassroots Football Report 2024](https://www.networldsports.co.uk/football/grassroots-report-2024)
- **Top challenge:** a Junior Grassroots survey of nearly 1,000 clubs found rising pitch hire costs were the number one challenge, followed by lack of sponsorship or funding. — [LoveAdmin / Thrive4Grassroots, club finances](https://loveadmin.com/football/grassroots-football-club-finances/)
- **Tech fragmentation:** "68% use three or more disconnected platforms" (claimed survey of 400 UK clubs). — [Teamstats blog](https://www.teamstats.net/blog/the-financial-realities-of-grassroots-football) (vendor blog, original survey not verified)
- **County FA budget template:** income lines are subs, sponsorship, donations and grants; spending lines are affiliation, pitch hire, coaches and referee fees. — [Lancashire FA club development plan template](https://thefa.com/-/media/cfa/lancashirefa/files/accreditation/two-and-three-star-club-development-plan.ashx)

### Inferences
- **Per-team running costs (estimate):** about £2,000-£5,000 a season for an adult 11-a-side team. That covers pitch £1.8k-£3.6k, referees £0.7k-£1.75k, affiliation and insurance about £150-£200, league fees, and kit amortisation. A multi-team youth club's turnover can reach tens of thousands of pounds.
- **Price as a share of budget:** £100-£300 a season is about 2-10% of a single-team club's budget. That is comparable to one or two referee fees a month at the low end, or one adult kit set at the high end.
- **Adult Sunday team match fees:** typically about £5-£10 per player per game. This range is from the brief; no current sourced survey was found, and the sources above give only youth weekly figures of £3-£5.

### Gaps
- No independent national survey of average club income or spending (FA, Sport England or Football Foundation) was found.
- No sourced current adult match-fee survey.
- No data on what clubs spend on websites or technology. Full FORZA PDFs might include this but could not be fetched.

## 4. What local businesses pay to sponsor a grassroots, Sunday league or step 7 club

### Takeaway
Most grassroots and Sunday sponsorship sits between £100 and £2,000 a season. Typical deals are a local trades or services business paying for a kit set (£250-£2,000), small packages such as a match ball (about £100), or advertising boards (£200-£500 a year). Larger multi-team clubs ask £500-£3,000 for headline packages. One outlier asks £15,000. Sponsorship is a near-universal income line (about 71-74% of clubs use it), and kits absorb about 93% of sponsorship use.

### Cited Findings
- **Kit sponsorship ranges:**
  - £500-£2,000 depending on squad size and whether training kit is included; £500-£1,000 for a youth team. — [Kit Funder shirt sponsorship guide](https://kitfunder.ai/blog-posts/shirt-sponsorship-guide); [Kit Funder: how to secure sponsors](https://kitfunder.ai/blog-posts/how-to-secure-sponsors-grassroots-team) (vendor)
  - Another platform puts the range at £250-£2,000. — [ClubHub UK](https://clubhubuk.co.uk/why-so-many-grassroots-kids-clubs-still-cant-afford-matching-kit/) (vendor)
  - Example quote: £630 for a full 16-player match kit for a season, plus £1,124 for training kit. — [Kit Funder](https://kitfunder.ai/blog-posts/shirt-sponsorship-guide)
- **Small packages:** a match ball sponsor at about £100, up to a main shirt sponsor at the top tier. — [Teamstats, local business partnerships](https://www.teamstats.net/blog/using-local-business-partnerships-to-fund-your-team)
- **Older consultant guidance:** sleeve, back or shorts sponsorship "£1,000-£3,000 per year" and advertising boards "£200-£500 per year per board". — [Kitlocker, sponsoring grassroots football](https://kitlocker.com/blog/sponsoring-grassroots-football) (older guide; figures likely apply to higher-level non-league clubs)
- **Real club rate cards:**
  - Wythenshawe FC 2026-27 deck: junior away-kit front-of-shirt £265-£850; club partner from £500; men's first-team back of shirt £3,000 and sleeve £1,500; veterans front of shirt listed at £15,000 (outlier, likely aspirational). — [Wythenshawe FC sponsorship deck 2026-27](https://wythenshaweafc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/WYTHENSHAW-FC-SPONSORSHIP-DECK-2026-27-2.pdf)
  - Player sponsorship packs at £200 each per season. — [Great Yarmouth Town FC sponsorship 2024-25](https://gytfc.co.uk/?p=4286)
  - Kit sponsor entry at £100. — [Southwater Royals sponsorship packages](https://www.southwaterroyals.uk/sponsorship-packages)
  - Kits sponsored at cost price: the main sponsor pays 2/3 and a secondary sponsor 1/3. — [Wellesbourne Wanderers 2023](https://www.wellesbournewanderersfc.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/2023-Sponsoring-Wellesbourne-Wanderers-2-1.pdf)
  - Knebworth FC also publishes sponsorship tiers. — [Knebworth FC sponsorship packages](https://knebworthfc.co.uk/sponsorship-packages/)
- **Typical sponsors:** local independent businesses such as builders, plumbers, estate agents, solicitors, dentists, driving instructors and restaurants. — [Kit Funder](https://kitfunder.ai/blog-posts/how-to-secure-sponsors-grassroots-team)
- **Usage:**
  - Sponsorship is one of the main fundraising methods for 71% of clubs (FORZA 2026) and 74% (FORZA 2024). — [FORZA 2026](https://www.forza.com/uk/football/grassroots-report); [FORZA 2024](https://www.networldsports.co.uk/football/grassroots-report-2024)
  - Sponsorship money goes to kits (93%), equipment (71-74%), pitch rental (28%) and facilities (27%). — [FORZA 2025](https://www.networldsports.co.uk/football/grassroots-report); [Spond summary of FORZA](https://www.spond.com/news-and-blog/grassroots-football-app-funding-crisis/)
- **Academic source:** a 2020 PhD thesis on sponsorship in grassroots football. — [Hindmarsh, LJMU 2020](https://researchonline.ljmu.ac.uk/id/eprint/13899/1/2020hindmarshphd.pdf) (not read in full)
- **Consultancy view:** clubs undervalue sponsorship and should sell audience and season-long exposure rather than a logo. — [SNAP Sponsorship](https://site.snapsponsorship.com/news/what-sponsorship-really-is-and-why-most-grassroots-clubs-undervalue-it)

### Inferences
- **Typical Sunday or adult county-league team (estimate):** one or two sponsors worth about £300-£1,000 a season in total, mostly paying for kit. Multi-team youth clubs and NLS step 5-6 clubs raise several thousand pounds across many packages.
- **App positioning (inference):** an app priced at £100-£300 a season is about the size of one small sponsor package. An app that creates sellable sponsor inventory (logos in the app, matchday posts, website or social exposure) could pitch itself as self-funding. This fits the finding that 71-74% of clubs already sell sponsorship and that clubs struggle to find sponsors.

### Gaps
- No Reddit or forum data could be retrieved; searches surfaced no usable threads.
- No independent average sponsorship value per club was found. FORZA reports give percentages, not pound values; the FORZA 2025 dataset has a truncated "£250+" item that could not be checked.

## 5. Evidence on willingness to pay for digital tools (Pitchero, Veo and others)

### Takeaway
Clubs do pay for digital tools, but the evidence on scale is thin and dated. Pitchero runs a freemium model with paid tiers at about £38-£100+ a month (third-party figures). Veo had over 1,500 UK customers by 2021, about 80% of them grassroots clubs or schools, on subscriptions of about £360 a year or more. Many County FAs promote Veo discounts. No source gave the share of clubs on paid Pitchero plans.

### Cited Findings
- **Pitchero pricing (third-party, sources conflict):**
  - Free tier plus Standard at about £38 a month. — [Hackceleration 2026 comparison](https://hackceleration.com/labs/best/sports-club-management-software)
  - £45-£100+ a month. — [TidyHQ compare page](https://tidyhq.com/compare/pitchero) (competitor)
  - Standard €44 a month (€484 a year) and Pro €115 a month (€1,265 a year). — [European Purpose](https://europeanpurpose.com/tool/pitchero)
  - Pitchero's own pages confirm a free plan with paid upgrades; payment fees run from 1.67% + 15p. — [Pitchero hockey page](https://pnd.pitchero.com/sports/hockey); [Pitchero payments](https://pnd.pitchero.com/features/payments)
- **Veo:**
  - Over 1,500 UK customers, about 80% of them grassroots clubs or schools (2021). — [Norfolk FA, 2021](https://www.norfolkfa.com/news/2021/mar/29/veo-partnership-renewed-until-2025)
  - More than 1,000 clubs using Veo (2020). — [Suffolk FA, 2020](https://www.suffolkfa.com/news/2020/jul/17/new-partnership-formed-with-veo)
  - Veo Starter subscription, aimed at grassroots clubs, at £360 a year for up to 5 games a month (2023). — [Shropshire FA, 2023](https://www.shropshirefa.com/news/2023/mar/14/veo); [Kent FA, 2023](https://www.kentfa.com/news/2023/mar/01/new-veo-starter-subscription---designed-for-grassroots-clubs)
  - County FA partnerships offering about £100-£200 camera discounts include London, Lancashire, Liverpool (Apr 2024), Sheffield (Jan 2024) and Gloucestershire (Nov 2023). — [Veo x London FA](https://www.veo.com/partnership/london-fa); [Liverpool FA, 2024](https://www.liverpoolfa.com/news/2024/apr/15/liverpool-fa-forms-partnership-with-veo); [Sheffield FA, 2024](https://www.sheffieldfa.com/news/2024/jan/22/sheffield-fa-x-veo); [Gloucestershire FA, 2023](https://www.gloucestershirefa.com/news/2023/nov/13/gfa-partners-with-veo-to-bring-match-recording-and-analysis-to-the-grassroots)
  - Pitchero and Veo have a partnership for streaming and analysis. — [Veo x Pitchero](https://www.veo.co/partnership/pitchero)
- **Fragmented tools:** clubs use several disconnected platforms; 68% use three or more (vendor-claimed survey). — [Teamstats](https://www.teamstats.net/blog/the-financial-realities-of-grassroots-football)
- **Spond is publicising grassroots funding problems:** its blog positions its free app against the funding crisis. — [Spond, FORZA grassroots report](https://www.spond.com/news-and-blog/grassroots-football-app-funding-crisis/)

### Inferences
- **Comparable pricing:** clubs already accept about £300-£1,200 a year for websites (Pitchero) and about £360+ a year for video (Veo). A £100-£300 a season app sits at or below existing paid tools. It also competes with free tools: Spond, the free FA Full-Time, and WhatsApp.
- **Rough market sizing (estimate):**
  - About 25,000 clubs × £100-£300 a season gives a theoretical club-level market of about £2.5m-£7.5m a year at 100% penetration.
  - Pricing per team instead (124,500 teams × £60-£360 a year, i.e. £5-£30 a month) gives about £7.5m-£45m a year at 100% penetration.
  - Realistic penetration of 2-10% implies roughly £0.1m-£4.5m in annual revenue.
  - Small adult Sunday and Saturday clubs are the most price-sensitive. Multi-team youth clubs have the most admin pain and the biggest budgets.

### Gaps
- No share of clubs on paid versus free Pitchero plans; Pitchero does not publish this.
- No current (2024-26) Veo UK customer count.
- No survey of club spending on technology or websites.
- No adoption figures for Spond, TeamApp or Teamo in England.
